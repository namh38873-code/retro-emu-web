Privacy Policy — Pixcade

Effective: 23 November 2026 · Pixcade is a retro game player. It does not include any commercial games.

What we collect

  • Your Google account (name, email address and profile picture) — only if you choose to sign in with Google. We use it to identify your account and to show your name and avatar in the app.
  • Your cloud saves, favorites and recently played games — stored on our server so you can keep playing on any device after signing in.

What stays on your device

  • Games you load yourself (ROM files) are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our server.
  • App settings (language, sound, controls, display) are stored only on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • Pixcade has no ads and no advertising trackers.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, or anything outside the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all of your data stored on our server at any time: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account, your cloud saves, your favorites and your recently played list. Games and settings stored only on your device are untouched — you can remove them by clearing the app data on your device. Deleting your account cannot be undone.

Children

Pixcade does not knowingly collect data from children. If you are a parent and believe your child signed in by mistake, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will note the new effective date here and mention the change in the app's update log.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: namh38873@gmail.com.
