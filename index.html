GAME LIBRARY

Free homebrew games shared by their authors — tap a game and it starts right here. No downloads, no installs.

Every library game is homebrew released for free by its author under the license shown on its card. RetroPlay does not include commercial ROMs — to play your own games, use Load your own ROM above and load a ROM file you own.

How to play

  1. Tap any game in the library — or tap Load your own ROM to load your own ROM file (.nes, .gba, or a .zip containing one). You can also drag & drop a file anywhere on this page.
  2. On phones, the touch gamepad appears automatically while a game is running. On desktop, use the arrow keys + Z / X / Enter / Shift.
  3. Use the player menu (bottom bar) for save states, screenshots and full screen. Tap ✕ Close to stop and go back to the library.
RetroPlay does not include any commercial games. Only load ROM files that you own and have the right to use.
