Load your own ROM

Play a game file from your device — .nes for NES, .gba for GBA, or a .zip containing one. You can also drag & drop a file anywhere on this page.

How to play

  1. Tap a game and it starts right here — on phones it goes full screen in landscape automatically.
  2. The touch gamepad appears while a game is running. On desktop, use the arrow keys + Z / X / Enter / Shift.
  3. Use the player menu (bottom bar of the player) for save states, screenshots and settings. Tap ✕ Close to stop.
RetroPlay does not include any commercial games. Only load ROM files that you own and have the right to use.
